# YUKIRA Constitution

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-14

---

## 1. Purpose

YUKIRA is an AI-powered Investment Intelligence Platform designed to help investors make better-informed investment decisions through rigorous analysis, evidence, verification, and explainability.

YUKIRA exists to provide the analytical checkpoint that an investor should use before meaningful capital is committed.

Its purpose is not to predict the future with certainty, generate blind investment tips, or optimize for engagement.

Its purpose is to **increase the probability of better investment decisions**.

---

## 2. Core Principles

YUKIRA is governed by the following principles:

1. **Evidence over opinion**  
   Investment conclusions must be grounded in verifiable evidence and clearly identified assumptions.

2. **Probability over prediction**  
   YUKIRA evaluates probabilities, scenarios, and uncertainty rather than claiming certainty about future outcomes.

3. **Risk before return**  
   The quality and sustainability of returns must be evaluated alongside downside risk, tail risk, and the conditions under which losses may occur.

4. **Verification before commitment**  
   YUKIRA exists as a checkpoint before meaningful investment decisions. Every important conclusion should invite one more question.

5. **Explainability before complexity**  
   Sophisticated analysis is valuable only when its reasoning can be understood, challenged, and independently examined.

6. **Transparency over marketing**  
   YUKIRA must clearly distinguish facts, calculations, assumptions, estimates, hypotheses, and judgments.

7. **Correctness over quantity**  
   A smaller number of reliable, decision-relevant insights is preferable to a larger number of poorly validated metrics.

8. **Long-term thinking over hype**  
   YUKIRA prioritizes durable investment characteristics over short-term narratives, popularity, or market excitement.

9. **No is a valid outcome**  
   YUKIRA must be willing to conclude that an investment should not be pursued when the evidence does not support it.

---

## 3. What YUKIRA Is

YUKIRA is a **decision-support and investment intelligence system**.

It combines:

- Deterministic financial calculations
- Quantitative analysis
- Risk and tail-risk analysis
- Factor analysis
- Portfolio analysis
- Valuation analysis
- Manager and investment-process analysis
- Portfolio-construction analysis
- Macro and regime analysis
- Scenario and stress testing
- Probabilistic forecasting
- Explainable artificial intelligence

YUKIRA transforms these inputs into structured investment intelligence intended to help investors understand:

- What an investment is
- How it has behaved
- Why it has behaved that way
- What risks it carries
- How resilient it may be under different conditions
- What assumptions support the conclusion
- What could invalidate the conclusion
- What the investor should investigate further

---

## 4. What YUKIRA Is Not

YUKIRA is not:

- A guaranteed prediction engine
- A blind investment-tip generator
- A substitute for investor judgment
- A system designed to maximize trading activity
- A system designed to manufacture confidence
- A black-box scoring system whose conclusions cannot be examined
- A mechanism for presenting speculation as fact

A YUKIRA conclusion must never be treated as certain merely because it is quantitative, algorithmic, or generated by artificial intelligence.

---

## 5. Investor-Centric Design

YUKIRA is built for **investors**.

The platform must support investors across a wide range of financial knowledge, experience, capital, objectives, and risk tolerance.

Complex financial concepts should be made understandable without sacrificing analytical rigor.

YUKIRA should progressively disclose information:

1. **Conclusion**
2. **Reasoning**
3. **Evidence**
4. **Risks**
5. **Uncertainty**
6. **Detailed analysis and methodology**

The objective is not to hide complexity, but to reveal it at the appropriate level.

---

## 6. Investment Decision Framework

YUKIRA must distinguish between different dimensions of an investment decision.

At minimum, the system should maintain separate concepts for:

### 6.1 Quality / Fund Score

How strong the underlying investment characteristics are based on the defined analytical framework.

### 6.2 Opportunity Score

Whether the current opportunity appears attractive relative to valuation, expectations, market conditions, and alternatives.

### 6.3 Investor Fit Score

How appropriate the investment may be for a particular investor's objectives, constraints, horizon, and risk capacity.

### 6.4 Confidence Score

How reliable the available evidence and resulting conclusion are.

These dimensions must not be collapsed into a single number without justification.

A high-quality investment is not automatically a good investment for every investor or at every price.

---

## 7. Decision Outcomes

YUKIRA may produce one of several decision states, including:

- **BUY**
- **HOLD**
- **WATCH**
- **INVESTIGATE**
- **REDUCE**
- **AVOID**

The system must not be forced to produce a positive investment conclusion.

**No investment is a legitimate outcome.**

Where evidence is insufficient, the appropriate conclusion may be uncertainty or investigation rather than a forced recommendation.

---

## 8. Hierarchical Analysis

YUKIRA must avoid treating every metric as an independent vote.

The decision engine should use a hierarchical analytical structure in which related metrics are grouped into meaningful dimensions.

Initial major dimensions include:

1. Return Quality
2. Risk & Tail Risk
3. Portfolio Quality
4. Manager Skill
5. Portfolio Construction
6. Valuation
7. Future Sustainability
8. Liquidity & Capacity
9. Governance & Operations
10. Macro, Scenario & Stress Resilience

Metrics must have a clearly defined purpose.

Correlated, redundant, or overlapping metrics must not be allowed to distort conclusions through accidental double-counting.

Complexity must not be mistaken for analytical quality.

---

## 9. Data Integrity

YUKIRA's conclusions are only as reliable as the data and methodology supporting them.

The system must actively guard against:

- Incorrect data
- Missing data
- Stale data
- Inconsistent data
- Data-source changes
- Benchmark changes
- Corporate or scheme actions affecting comparability
- Survivorship bias
- Look-ahead bias
- Selection bias
- Period-definition errors
- Calculation errors
- Unexplained data transformations

Data validation must occur before important calculations are used by the decision engine.

Where material data limitations exist, they must be surfaced rather than hidden.

---

## 10. Quantitative Integrity

Numerical calculations must be performed by deterministic and testable software.

Examples include:

- Returns
- CAGR
- Rolling returns
- Volatility
- Drawdowns
- Risk-adjusted measures
- Portfolio statistics
- Factor exposures
- Valuation metrics
- Scenario calculations
- Statistical measures

Artificial intelligence must not silently replace validated numerical calculations.

AI may interpret, explain, compare, synthesize, or investigate quantitative results, but it must not invent financial facts or override validated calculations without an explicit and auditable process.

---

## 11. AI Governance

Artificial intelligence is a component of YUKIRA, not the unquestioned authority of YUKIRA.

AI may be used to:

- Research
- Retrieve and organize information
- Interpret financial information
- Identify anomalies
- Synthesize evidence
- Generate explanations
- Compare qualitative factors
- Produce investor-facing reports

AI must not:

- Invent financial facts
- Fabricate sources
- Manufacture evidence
- Present assumptions as facts
- Override deterministic calculations without justification
- Conceal uncertainty
- Create false precision

Important AI-generated conclusions must remain traceable to the evidence and analytical processes supporting them.

---

## 12. Risk and Uncertainty

YUKIRA must treat uncertainty as a first-class analytical concept.

Every meaningful investment conclusion should consider:

- What could go right
- What could go wrong
- How severe the downside could be
- Which assumptions are most important
- Which risks are already reflected in valuation
- Which risks may be underestimated
- What conditions could invalidate the thesis

YUKIRA must avoid presenting probabilistic analysis as certainty.

The system should explicitly communicate when evidence is weak, conflicting, incomplete, or highly regime-dependent.

---

## 13. Scenario and Stress Analysis

Historical performance alone is insufficient to establish investment resilience.

Where appropriate, YUKIRA should evaluate investments under multiple scenarios, including:

- Normal conditions
- Adverse market conditions
- Severe drawdowns
- Changes in interest rates
- Inflation shocks
- Liquidity stress
- Sector or factor rotations
- Changes in market regime
- Other investment-specific stress conditions

Scenario analysis must clearly distinguish between historical evidence, model assumptions, and hypothetical outcomes.

---

## 14. Model Risk

YUKIRA must actively guard against model risk.

The platform must recognize the possibility of:

- Overfitting
- Metric redundancy
- Data-mining bias
- Regime changes
- Structural breaks
- False statistical relationships
- Unstable parameters
- Inadequate sample sizes
- Benchmark contamination
- Look-ahead bias
- Survivorship bias
- Poor calibration
- Misleading historical relationships

Predictive claims must be validated through appropriate testing and, where applicable, backtesting.

Historical success must never be treated as proof of future success.

---

## 15. Auditability and Traceability

Every important YUKIRA output should eventually be traceable through a chain such as:

**Investment Conclusion → Score → Methodology Version → Metric Values → Calculations → Input Data → Data Source → Timestamp → Software/Model Version**

The system should preserve sufficient information to reconstruct how an important conclusion was produced.

Changes to methodology, scoring logic, assumptions, and important data transformations must be versioned.

---

## 16. Evidence Classification

YUKIRA should distinguish clearly between different types of information.

At minimum:

- **Fact** — supported by an identifiable source or validated dataset.
- **Calculation** — deterministically derived from defined inputs and formulas.
- **Assumption** — an explicitly stated premise used in analysis.
- **Estimate** — an approximation derived from available information.
- **Hypothesis** — a proposition that requires validation.
- **Judgment** — an analytical interpretation based on evidence and reasoning.
- **Scenario** — a conditional or hypothetical outcome.

These categories must not be presented interchangeably.

---

## 17. Methodological Discipline

Every important metric should have:

- A defined purpose
- A precise definition
- A documented formula or methodology
- Defined inputs
- Defined data requirements
- Appropriate interpretation
- Known limitations
- Validation requirements
- A defined relationship to the decision framework

YUKIRA should not add metrics merely because they are available or sophisticated.

A metric earns a place in the system by demonstrating decision relevance.

---

## 18. Progressive Development

YUKIRA will not attempt to build its complete analytical system immediately.

The initial product should prioritize:

1. Correctness
2. Data integrity
3. Methodological soundness
4. Auditability
5. Maintainability
6. Usefulness
7. Breadth
8. Visual polish

The MVP should contain a deliberately limited set of high-value metrics supported by real data, persistence, scoring, testing, and explainable reporting.

The architecture should allow the analytical framework to expand over time without requiring fundamental redesign.

---

## 19. Transparency of Limitations

YUKIRA must communicate material limitations openly.

If the system lacks:

- Sufficient data
- Reliable historical observations
- Appropriate benchmarks
- Adequate sample size
- Confidence in a calculation
- Confidence in a forecast
- Sufficient information about an investment

then the limitation should influence the conclusion and, where material, be shown to the investor.

The system must prefer **honest uncertainty over manufactured confidence**.

---

## 20. Investor Conclusion Standard

Every meaningful YUKIRA investment assessment should answer, in understandable language:

1. **What does YUKIRA think?**
2. **Why does YUKIRA think this?**
3. **What evidence supports the conclusion?**
4. **What are the key risks?**
5. **What could make YUKIRA wrong?**
6. **What should the investor investigate next?**

A score without reasoning is insufficient.

A recommendation without evidence is insufficient.

A sophisticated model without explainability is insufficient.

---

## 21. Ethical and Product Standard

YUKIRA must prioritize the long-term interests of investors over short-term engagement or commercial incentives.

The platform should not intentionally:

- Manufacture urgency
- Encourage unnecessary trading
- Exploit investor fear or greed
- Hide material risks
- Overstate analytical confidence
- Present marketing claims as research
- Optimize recommendations for transaction volume

Trust is a core product asset.

Reputation is more important than engagement.

---

## 22. Evolution of the Constitution

This Constitution is a foundational document, not a substitute for detailed methodology or technical specifications.

Detailed definitions, formulas, schemas, architectures, research findings, scoring systems, data-source specifications, validation procedures, and implementation decisions should be maintained in their appropriate YUKIRA project documents.

Changes to this Constitution must be deliberate, documented, and version-controlled.

The Constitution should evolve only when experience, research, or fundamental changes to YUKIRA justify the change.

---

## 23. North Star

YUKIRA exists to:

> **Increase the probability of better investment decisions.**

Every major product, research, engineering, and analytical decision should ultimately be evaluated against this objective.

YUKIRA should become the trusted **double-check** that investors instinctively use before making meaningful investment decisions.

---

**End of Constitution**
